## What it is
A private study site for one learner: a Korean student preparing for Japanese pharmacy-school chemistry (Japanese curriculum, Japanese terminology). It holds
1. **합격화학 연습문제 유기편 해설** - 7 chapters (31-37), 350 problems, each with official answer, 문제 요지, 핵심 개념, 풀이, 선지별 해설, 포인트, RDKit structure drawings.
2. **무기화학 정리 교재** - Korean rewrite of her own Japanese summary notes (18-page PDF + two reference tables), with terms kept in Japanese.

## Who and where
One reader, phone first (KakaoTalk link), also a laptop at a desk during evening study. Reads long explanations, checks answers after solving on paper, drills colors / gas reactions for memorization.

## Must do
- Find a problem by chapter + number in seconds; see the answer before deciding to open the explanation.
- Read long Korean explanations comfortably (Japanese terms and hanja inline).
- Browse the inorganic notes by topic; self-test memorization tables (colors, flame tests, gas preparation).
- Remember progress locally (checked / starred) - no account. (assumption)

## Must not
- No login, no tracking, noindex (private material from a commercial workbook).
- No invented content; textbook facts come from her notes and the existing reviewed explanations.

## Tone
Calm, focused, trustworthy tutor. Not gamified, not childish.
